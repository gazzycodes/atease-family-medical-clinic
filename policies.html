Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA)

Effective date: to be confirmed

This notice describes how your medical information may be used and disclosed, and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

Our legal duty

AtEase Family Medical Clinic is required by federal and applicable Texas law to:

  • Maintain the privacy of your protected health information (PHI).
  • Provide you with this Notice of Privacy Practices.
  • Follow the terms of the current notice.
  • Notify you if a breach of your unsecured protected health information occurs when required by law.

How we may use and disclose your health information

Treatment — to provide, coordinate and manage your healthcare. For example: diagnosing medical conditions, prescribing medications, ordering laboratory testing, referring you to specialists, and telemedicine services.

Payment — to obtain payment for healthcare services. For example: billing insurance companies, collecting patient balances, verifying insurance eligibility, and obtaining prior authorization.

Healthcare operations — to improve the quality and efficiency of our practice. For example: quality improvement, staff training, credentialing, licensing, compliance activities and audits.

Other permitted uses

We may disclose information when required or permitted by law, including:

  • Public health reporting
  • Reporting abuse or neglect when legally required
  • Law enforcement requests
  • Court orders
  • Workers' compensation claims
  • Health oversight agencies
  • Organ and tissue donation
  • Medical examiners or funeral directors
  • Disaster relief efforts

Uses requiring your written authorization

We will obtain your written authorization before:

  • Using your information for most marketing purposes
  • Selling your health information
  • Disclosing psychotherapy notes (except as permitted by law)
  • Other uses not otherwise permitted by HIPAA

You may revoke your authorization at any time in writing, except where action has already been taken.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Inspect and obtain copies of your medical records (subject to applicable law)
  • Request corrections or amendments to your records
  • Request restrictions on certain uses and disclosures
  • Request confidential communications by alternative means or locations
  • Receive an accounting of certain disclosures
  • Receive a paper copy of this notice at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically

Texas privacy protections

Texas law may provide additional protections for certain health information beyond federal HIPAA requirements. AtEase Family Medical Clinic complies with applicable federal and Texas privacy laws regarding the use and disclosure of your medical information.

Questions or complaints

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may contact our Privacy Officer at AtEase Family Medical Clinic — 682-297-3822, info@ateasefamilymedicalclinic.com.

You may also file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. Filing a complaint will not affect your care or result in retaliation.

Changes to this notice

We reserve the right to change this notice at any time. Revised notices will be posted in the clinic, made available upon request, and published on this page.

Financial Policy

Thank you for choosing AtEase Family Medical Clinic. Payment is expected at the time services are rendered unless prior arrangements have been made.

Insurance

  • We participate with many insurance plans.
  • Patients are responsible for verifying coverage.
  • Insurance verification does not guarantee payment.
  • Copays, deductibles and coinsurance are due at the time of service.

Self-pay patients

Payment is due at the time of the visit unless another arrangement has been approved. Our published cash prices cover the consultation itself; laboratory tests, imaging, medications and referrals are billed separately by the provider supplying them.

If you are uninsured or choosing not to use insurance, you are entitled to a written Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before a scheduled service. Ask us and we will provide one.

Outstanding balances

Balances remaining after insurance processing are due upon receipt of the statement.

Returned checks

Returned checks may be subject to an additional processing fee.

Missed appointments

Appointments not cancelled at least 24 hours in advance may be subject to a missed appointment fee. Repeated missed appointments may result in dismissal from the practice.

Forms

Completion of disability forms, FMLA paperwork, prior authorizations and other administrative forms may require additional fees.

Collections

Accounts that become significantly past due may be referred to a collection agency in accordance with applicable law.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Our goal is to provide timely, high-quality care to every patient. If you cannot keep your appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance.

Missed appointments

Failure to provide adequate notice may result in:

  • A missed appointment fee — amount to be confirmed
  • Delayed scheduling of future appointments
  • Possible dismissal from the practice after repeated missed appointments

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to reschedule, depending on the provider's schedule.

Repeated cancellations or no-shows interfere with patient care and may affect your ability to remain an active patient with the practice.

Thank you for respecting the time of our providers and fellow patients.

Forms You'll Be Asked to Sign

These are completed and signed securely in your patient portal — not on this website — so that a signed copy is stored in your medical record. You'll be prompted for the ones that apply to you when you book, and before your visit begins.

  • Telemedicine Consent Explains how virtual visits work, their benefits and limits, your responsibilities during a session, and what to do in an emergency.
  • General Consent for Treatment Your consent to evaluation, testing and treatment, and assignment of insurance benefits.
  • Financial Policy Acknowledgement Confirms you've read and agree to the Financial Policy above.
  • Cancellation Policy Acknowledgement Confirms you've read and agree to the Cancellation & No-Show Policy above.
  • Authorization to Communicate Lets you choose how we contact you about appointments, lab results and prescriptions.
  • Notice of Privacy Practices Acknowledgement Confirms you've received the privacy notice above.
  • GLP-1 Weight Management Consent Only if you are prescribed compounded GLP-1 medication. Covers contraindications, side effects, directions for use, and the nature of compounded medications.
In an emergency, call 911. A virtual visit is not for emergencies. If you are experiencing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke symptoms, severe abdominal pain, heavy bleeding, or any other medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
