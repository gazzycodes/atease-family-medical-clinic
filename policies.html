Notice of Privacy Practices (HIPAA)

Original effective date: 1 August 2026

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can access this information. Please review it carefully.

Our responsibilities

AtEase Family Medical Clinic is required by federal and applicable Texas law to:

  • Maintain the privacy and security of your protected health information (“PHI”).
  • Provide you with this Notice of Privacy Practices.
  • Follow the duties and privacy practices described in the notice currently in effect.
  • Notify you promptly, as required by law, if a breach occurs that may have compromised the privacy or security of your information.
  • Refrain from using or disclosing your information except as described in this notice or otherwise permitted or required by law.

How we may use and disclose your health information

Treatment — we may use and disclose your health information to provide, coordinate, and manage your healthcare. Examples include:

  • Diagnosing and treating medical conditions.
  • Prescribing medications.
  • Ordering laboratory testing or diagnostic imaging.
  • Referring you to specialists.
  • Coordinating your care with other healthcare professionals.
  • Providing telehealth or virtual-visit services.

Payment — we may use and disclose your health information to bill and obtain payment for healthcare services. Examples include:

  • Billing insurance companies or health plans.
  • Verifying insurance coverage and eligibility.
  • Obtaining prior authorization.
  • Collecting copayments, deductibles, coinsurance, and other patient balances.
  • Responding to questions concerning claims or payment.

Healthcare operations — we may use and disclose your health information to operate our practice, improve your care, and maintain the quality and efficiency of our services. Examples include:

  • Quality assessment and improvement activities.
  • Staff training and performance evaluation.
  • Credentialing and licensing.
  • Compliance activities.
  • Audits.
  • Business management and administrative services.
  • Contacting you when necessary regarding your care or our services.

Other permitted or required uses and disclosures

We may use or disclose your health information when permitted or required by law, including for:

  • Public health and safety activities.
  • Preventing or controlling disease.
  • Reporting adverse reactions to medications or problems with medical products.
  • Reporting suspected abuse, neglect, or domestic violence when legally required.
  • Preventing or reducing a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.
  • Health research as permitted by law.
  • Health-oversight activities.
  • Workers’ compensation claims.
  • Law-enforcement purposes.
  • Court or administrative orders, subpoenas, lawsuits, and other legal proceedings.
  • Organ and tissue donation.
  • Coroners, medical examiners, and funeral directors.
  • Disaster-relief activities.
  • Special government functions, including military, national security, and protective services.
  • Compliance with federal and state laws and requests from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.

Substance-use-disorder records

To the extent that AtEase Family Medical Clinic receives or maintains substance-use-disorder patient records protected by 42 CFR Part 2, we will not use or disclose information from those records in a civil, criminal, administrative, or legislative investigation or proceeding against you without:

  1. Your written consent; or
  2. A court order accompanied by a subpoena or other legal requirement compelling disclosure.

Additional protections may apply to substance-use-disorder treatment records under federal and state law.

Uses and disclosures requiring written authorization

We will obtain your written authorization before:

  • Using your information for most marketing purposes.
  • Selling your health information.
  • Disclosing most psychotherapy notes.
  • Making other uses or disclosures not described in this notice or otherwise permitted or required by law.

AtEase Family Medical Clinic does not sell your health information.

You may revoke an authorization at any time by submitting a written request. Revocation will not affect actions already taken in reliance on the authorization.

Your choices

In certain situations, you may tell us your preferences regarding how we share your information. These may include:

  • Sharing information with family members, close friends, caregivers, or others involved in your care or payment for your care.
  • Sharing information during disaster-relief activities.
  • Contacting you about appointment reminders, treatment alternatives, or health-related services.

If you are unable to communicate your preference, such as during an emergency, we may disclose information when we believe it is in your best interest or when necessary to reduce a serious and imminent threat to health or safety.

Your rights

You have the right to:

Access your records. You may inspect or obtain an electronic or paper copy of your medical record and other health information maintained by AtEase Family Medical Clinic, subject to applicable law. We generally will provide the requested information or a summary within 30 days. We may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee when permitted by law.

Request an amendment. You may ask us to correct health information that you believe is incorrect or incomplete. We may deny your request in certain circumstances, but we will provide the reason for the denial in writing, generally within 60 days.

Request confidential communications. You may ask us to contact you in a specific way or at a different address. We will accommodate reasonable requests.

Request restrictions. You may ask us not to use or disclose certain information for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations. We are generally not required to agree to the request. If you pay for a healthcare service or item completely out of pocket, you may ask us not to disclose information about that service or item to your health plan for payment or healthcare-operation purposes. We will honor the request unless the law requires disclosure.

Receive an accounting of disclosures. You may request a list of certain disclosures of your health information made during the six years before your request. The list will not include disclosures made for treatment, payment, healthcare operations, or certain other disclosures excluded by law. One accounting during any 12 months will be provided without charge. A reasonable, cost-based fee may apply to additional requests.

Obtain a copy of this notice. You may request a paper copy of this notice at any time, even if you agreed to receive it electronically.

Choose a personal representative. If another person has legal authority to act on your behalf, such as a legal guardian or someone with a valid medical power of attorney, that person may exercise your privacy rights. We may verify that person’s authority before taking action.

File a complaint. You may file a complaint if you believe your privacy rights have been violated. AtEase Family Medical Clinic will not retaliate against you or affect your care because you filed a complaint.

Texas privacy protections

Texas law may provide protections for certain medical, mental-health, genetic, communicable-disease, and other sensitive health information that are more restrictive than federal HIPAA requirements.

When Texas or another applicable law provides greater privacy protection, AtEase Family Medical Clinic will follow the more protective requirement.

Other notice and information for patients

  • Appointment fees are due before service unless other arrangements have been made.
  • Medicare and select commercial insurance plans are accepted. Insurance coverage depends on eligibility, medical necessity, and your health plan’s terms.
  • Copayments, deductibles, coinsurance, and non-covered services are the patient’s responsibility.
  • Verification of insurance benefits does not guarantee payment. Patients are responsible for charges not paid by their insurance plan.
  • Self-pay prices apply to patients who choose not to use insurance or whose services are not covered.
  • Prescription refills and medication changes are provided only when clinically appropriate. A visit does not guarantee that a medication will be prescribed or refilled.
  • Controlled substances are not prescribed through telehealth visits.
  • Patients must be physically located in a state where the healthcare provider is licensed at the time of the telehealth visit.
  • Telehealth services are not appropriate for medical emergencies. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
  • Appointment availability, visit length, treatment recommendations, and fees are subject to change.

Cancellation policy: Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be subject to a fee.

By scheduling an appointment, the patient acknowledges and agrees to the clinic’s financial, privacy, telehealth, cancellation, and treatment policies.

Changes to this notice

We reserve the right to change the terms of this notice and make the revised notice effective for all health information we maintain, including information created or received before the revision.

If we make a material change, the revised notice will be available upon request, posted at the clinic, and posted on our website if we maintain a website describing our healthcare services.

Financial Policy

Thank you for choosing AtEase Family Medical Clinic. Payment is expected at the time services are rendered unless prior arrangements have been made.

Insurance

  • We participate with many insurance plans.
  • Patients are responsible for verifying coverage.
  • Insurance verification does not guarantee payment.
  • Copays, deductibles and coinsurance are due at the time of service.

Self-pay patients

Payment is due at the time of the visit unless another arrangement has been approved. Our published cash prices cover the consultation itself; laboratory tests, imaging, medications and referrals are billed separately by the provider supplying them.

If you are uninsured or choosing not to use insurance, you are entitled to a written Good Faith Estimate of expected charges before a scheduled service. Ask us and we will provide one.

Outstanding balances

Balances remaining after insurance processing are due upon receipt of the statement.

Returned checks

Returned checks may be subject to an additional processing fee.

Missed appointments

Appointments not cancelled at least 24 hours in advance may be subject to a missed appointment fee. Repeated missed appointments may result in dismissal from the practice.

Forms

Completion of disability forms, FMLA paperwork, prior authorizations and other administrative forms may require additional fees.

Collections

Accounts that become significantly past due may be referred to a collection agency in accordance with applicable law.

Cancellation & No-Show Policy

Our goal is to provide timely, high-quality care to every patient. If you cannot keep your appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance.

Missed appointments

Failure to provide adequate notice may result in:

  • A missed appointment fee of $25
  • Delayed scheduling of future appointments
  • Possible dismissal from the practice after repeated missed appointments

Patients arriving more than 15 minutes late may be asked to reschedule, depending on the provider's schedule.

Repeated cancellations or no-shows interfere with patient care and may affect your ability to remain an active patient with the practice.

Thank you for respecting the time of our providers and fellow patients.
